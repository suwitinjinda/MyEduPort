## University Categorization Logic

The MyEduPort system automatically categorizes universities into three categories based on selectivity and student profile: **Reach**, **Target**, and **Safety**. This categorization helps students understand their admission chances and build a balanced application list.

### Categorization Rules

#### Primary Categorization (Based on Selectivity Band)

1. **Very High Selectivity** → **Reach**
   - Match Percentage: 25-40%
   - Explanation: "Top-tier research, highly competitive"
   - These are the most prestigious and competitive universities
   - Examples: Harvard, MIT, Stanford, Oxford, Cambridge, Tsinghua

2. **Medium Selectivity** → **Safety**
   - Match Percentage: 60-80%
   - Explanation: "Good match, accessible admission"
   - More accessible universities with good admission chances
   - Examples: University of Exeter, Lancaster University

3. **High Selectivity** → **Distributed between Target and Safety**
   - **50% Target**: Match Percentage 50-80%, Explanation: "Strong match, competitive admission"
   - **50% Safety**: Match Percentage 60-80%, Explanation: "Good match, accessible admission"
   - This ensures balanced distribution and provides Safety options
   - Examples: University of Michigan, NYU, University of Manchester

#### Distribution Guarantees

The system ensures:
- **Minimum Safety Universities**: At least 20% of total recommendations (minimum 2 universities)
- **Balanced Distribution**: If Safety count is too low, some "High" selectivity universities are automatically converted to Safety
- **Fair Representation**: All three categories are represented in recommendations

#### GPA-Based Adjustments

The categorization may be adjusted based on student GPA:

1. **Low GPA (< 3.0)**:
   - Reach universities may be downgraded to Target
   - Match percentage adjusted to 40-60%

2. **High GPA (> 3.8)**:
   - Up to 30% of Safety universities may be upgraded to Target
   - Maintains balance while recognizing strong academic performance
   - Match percentage adjusted to 55-80%

3. **Normal GPA (3.0-3.8)**:
   - Standard categorization applies
   - No adjustments made

### Match Percentage Ranges

| Category | Match Percentage | Meaning |
|----------|-----------------|---------|
| **Reach** | 25-40% | Challenging admission, but worth applying if you meet minimum requirements |
| **Target** | 50-80% | Good match, admission is likely with your credentials |
| **Safety** | 60-80% | Strong match, high probability of admission |

### How Categories Are Used

1. **Reach Universities (25-40% match)**:
   - Dream schools that are highly competitive
   - Apply to 2-3 reach schools
   - Requires exceptional academic and extracurricular profile
   - Strong essays and recommendations critical

2. **Target Universities (50-80% match)**:
   - Best fit universities based on your profile
   - Apply to 4-6 target schools
   - Good balance of competitiveness and admission likelihood
   - Core of your application list

3. **Safety Universities (60-80% match)**:
   - Universities where admission is very likely
   - Apply to 2-3 safety schools
   - Ensures you have admission options
   - Should still be universities you'd be happy to attend

### Example Distribution

For a typical recommendation list of 30 universities:
- **Reach**: ~8-10 universities (27-33%)
- **Target**: ~12-15 universities (40-50%)
- **Safety**: ~6-8 universities (20-27%)

### Important Notes

1. **Dynamic Categorization**: Categories are calculated in real-time based on:
   - University selectivity band
   - Student's GPA (if provided)
   - Total number of universities in results
   - Distribution balance requirements

2. **Country-Specific Considerations**:
   - **US Universities**: Categorization considers SAT/ACT scores, GPA, and extracurriculars
   - **UK Universities**: Categorization considers A-Levels/IB scores and personal statements
   - **China Universities**: Categorization considers Gaokao scores and academic performance

3. **Major-Specific Variations**:
   - Some majors may be more competitive than others at the same university
   - The system provides general categorization; specific programs may vary

4. **Regular Updates**:
   - Categorization logic is reviewed and updated based on admission trends
   - Match percentages are estimates based on historical data

### Technical Implementation

The categorization logic is implemented in `backend/src/services/aiClient.js` in the `categorizeUniversities()` function. The algorithm:

1. **First Pass**: Categorizes based on selectivity_band
2. **Distribution Check**: Ensures minimum Safety universities
3. **GPA Adjustment**: Refines categories based on student GPA
4. **Balance Guarantee**: Converts universities if needed to maintain balance

This ensures students always receive a balanced, realistic set of university recommendations across all three categories.
